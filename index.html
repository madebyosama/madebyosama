Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before we start.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form or book a call and tell me about your business and your current site. I usually reply within a few hours.

Can you rebuild my existing website?

Yes, that's most of what I do. I rebuild slow, cluttered sites from scratch so they load fast, work on every screen and are easy to update, and your existing content comes across.

Which package should I choose?

If you need one page that sells one thing, like a service, a launch or an offer, go with the landing page. If you need several pages or want to edit content yourself, go with multiple pages. Not sure? Tell me about the project and I'll recommend one.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. The multiple pages plan includes a CMS, so you can change text, images and posts without waiting on a developer.

How many revisions do I get?

The design happens in Figma before anything is built, and you give feedback at every step through our Slack channel. We refine it together until you're happy, then I build it.

I already have a design. Can you build it?

Yes. Send me the Figma file and I'll build it responsive, fast and true to the design.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. Agencies have trusted me with design and WordPress builds for their clients for years, and I'm happy to work under your brand.

What happens after launch?

The site is yours. If you need changes, new pages or help later on, I'm a message away.
