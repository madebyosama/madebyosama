Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before we start.

How do I get started?

Request a free growth audit or book a call. Tell me about your business and your goals, and I'll reply within a few hours with what I'd do first.

What's included in the free growth audit?

I review your website, how you show up on Google and your social media, then send you a short, prioritized list of the changes that would bring in the most customers. There's no obligation to hire me.

How fast will I see results?

Quick wins, like fixing your website or your Google Business Profile, can show results within weeks. Ranking higher on Google builds over three to six months and keeps paying off. I start with the fastest wins, so you see progress early.

Do I need a new website?

Usually not. Most websites can be fixed as they are. If yours is holding you back, I'll tell you honestly after the audit, and I can rebuild it.

Do you run ads?

Yes, when they make sense for your business. I set up and manage Google and Meta ads, and the ad budget is paid directly to Google or Meta, separate from my fee.

How do you report results?

Every month you get a plain-English report: what I did, what changed in visitors, enquiries and customers, and what's next. Day-to-day updates come through a dedicated Slack channel.
