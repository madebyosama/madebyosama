Bookshelf

Books that shaped how I work.

  • Atomic Habits

    James Clear

    Tiny changes, remarkable results. The book that got me obsessive about small, compounding improvements.

  • The Design of Everyday Things

    Don Norman

    Required reading for anyone who designs anything people have to use. Changed how I look at every button and door handle.

  • Steve Jobs

    Walter Isaacson

    Equal parts inspiring and cautionary. A masterclass in obsessing over the details no one else notices.

  • Sapiens

    Yuval Noah Harari

    A history of everything that somehow makes building software feel like a very small, very recent idea.

  • Deep Work

    Cal Newport

    My playbook for shutting out the noise and actually shipping things.

  • Show Your Work!

    Austin Kleon

    The permission slip I needed to start sharing process, not just polished results.
