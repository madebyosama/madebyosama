AEO, or answer engine optimization, is how your business becomes part of the answer when someone asks an AI tool. When a customer asks ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews or Perplexity for a recommendation, AEO is the work that gets your business mentioned. It's included in my SEO & AEO plans, from **$500 a month**.

## What is AEO?

AEO is optimizing your website and online presence so AI tools understand what you do, trust it, and quote or recommend you in their answers.

Classic [SEO](/seo-services) is about ranking as a link on a results page. AEO is about being the source an AI tool summarizes or the business it names. They share the same foundations, but AEO puts more weight on clear, direct answers, structured data and a consistent reputation across the web.

You may also see it called GEO (generative engine optimization) or LLM optimization. They're names for the same idea.

## Why does AEO matter for small businesses?

AI answers usually name only a handful of businesses, sometimes just one. If you're not one of them, the customer may never reach a list of links at all.

The upside: many small businesses haven't started on this yet, so a well-structured site with clear answers can stand out, even against bigger competitors.

## What does AEO work include?

- **Question research:** the questions your customers ask AI tools about your service, your area and your competitors.
- **Answer-first content:** pages and FAQs that open with a clear, direct answer, the kind AI tools can quote word for word.
- **Structured data (schema):** markup that tells machines who you are, what you offer, your prices, reviews and FAQs.
- **Consistent business details:** the same name, description and details across your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories, so AI tools are confident they're talking about you.
- **Reviews and mentions:** a plan for earning reviews and mentions on sites that AI tools trust.
- **Crawler access:** making sure AI crawlers can actually read your site, and nothing blocks them by accident.
- **Monitoring:** checking what the main AI tools say about your business each month, and fixing what's wrong or missing.

## How is AEO different from SEO?

| | SEO | AEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal | Rank as a link on Google | Be quoted or recommended in AI answers |
| Where it shows | Search results pages | ChatGPT, AI Overviews, Perplexity, voice assistants |
| What matters most | Keywords, links, technical health | Direct answers, structured data, consistent reputation |
| How it's measured | Rankings, clicks, traffic | Mentions in AI answers, AI referral traffic |

In practice you want both, which is why they come as one plan.

## Questions

### Is AEO the same as GEO?

Mostly, yes. AEO (answer engine optimization), GEO (generative engine optimization) and LLM optimization all describe making your business visible in AI-generated answers.

### Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. AI tools decide their own answers, and they change often. I can make your business much easier for them to understand, trust and quote, and show you each month where you're being mentioned.

### Do I need SEO before AEO?

They work best together. AI tools lean heavily on search results and on sites that are fast, well structured and clearly written. That's why every plan includes both.

### How do I know if AEO is working?

Each month I check a set of your most important questions in the main AI tools and report which ones mention you. You'll also see visits that come from AI tools in your analytics.
