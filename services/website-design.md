- **Custom design in Figma:** you see and approve the design before anything is built.
- **Responsive build:** looks and works right on phones, tablets and desktops.
- **SEO-ready foundations:** fast pages, clean structure, proper titles and descriptions, structured data (schema) and analytics set up from day one.
- **Easy updates:** multi-page sites include a CMS, so you can change text, images and posts yourself.
- **Clear communication:** a dedicated Slack channel and regular updates. Meetings are optional.

A landing page takes about two weeks. A multi-page website takes two to six weeks, depending on the number of pages and features.

Yes. Send me the Figma file and I'll build it responsive, fast and true to the design.

### Will my new website be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site I build is fast, mobile-friendly and properly structured for search engines, with titles, descriptions and schema in place. For ongoing ranking work, see [SEO services](/seo-services).

### How many revisions do I get?

The design happens in Figma before anything is built, and you give feedback at every step. We refine it together until you're happy, then I build it.

The site is yours. You can add a care plan for updates and maintenance, or message me whenever you need changes or new pages.
