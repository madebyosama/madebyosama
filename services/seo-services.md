I help small businesses rank higher on Google, so the right customers find them without paying for every click. SEO & AEO plans start at **$500 a month**, and every month you get a clear report showing what I did and what changed.

## What do SEO services include?

SEO (search engine optimization) is everything that helps your website show up when people search for what you sell. My plans cover the whole job, not just keywords:

- **SEO audit:** a full check of your site's technical health, content and competitors, so we know exactly what's holding your rankings back.
- **Keyword research:** the searches your customers actually type, sorted by how likely they are to turn into enquiries or sales.
- **Technical SEO:** page speed, mobile problems, indexing, broken links, redirects and structured data (schema), fixed at the source.
- **On-page SEO:** titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and page copy written for people first and search engines second.
- **Content:** new service pages and articles that answer the questions your customers are asking.
- **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile setup and optimization, local citations, location pages and a simple plan for getting more reviews, so you show up in Google Maps and "near me" searches.
- **Tracking:** Google Search Console and GA4 set up properly, so you can see which searches and pages bring customers in.

## Who are these SEO services for?

Small businesses, local service providers, agencies and online stores that have a website but don't get enough visitors or enquiries from Google. It's also a good fit if your site is new or was just rebuilt and needs to start ranking.

If most of your customers come from referrals today, SEO is how you add a steady second channel that keeps working when you're busy.

## How long does SEO take to work?

Usually two to three months for the first clear movement, and four to six months for results you can feel in enquiries or sales. Technical fixes can help within weeks. Competitive keywords take longer, because you're overtaking sites that have been working on them for years.

SEO builds on itself. Pages that rank keep bringing in visitors after the work is done, which is why it usually costs less per customer over time than ads.
