I help online stores sell more from the visitors they already have, then bring in more of the right visitors. Ecommerce growth plans start at **$800 a month**. Ad budgets are paid directly to Google or Meta and are separate from my fee.

## What does ecommerce growth include?

- **Store audit and research:** where visitors drop off, what competitors do better and what your customers care about.
- **Conversion rate optimization (CRO):** clearer product pages, better photos and copy, trust signals, faster pages and a smoother checkout, so more visitors buy.
- **Email marketing:** welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back flows that run on their own, plus regular campaigns.
- **Paid ads:** Google Search and Shopping ads and Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram, built around products that already sell.
- **Ecommerce SEO:** product and category pages optimized for search, with product and review schema. See [SEO services](/seo-services).
- **Analytics and tracking:** GA4 ecommerce tracking and ad conversion tracking set up properly, and a monthly report you can actually read.

## Why start with conversion before ads?

Because ads multiply whatever your store already does. If only one in a hundred visitors buys, paying for more visitors is expensive. Fixing product pages, checkout and email first means every ad dollar works harder later.

## Which store platforms do you work with?

I work with WordPress and WooCommerce stores and custom-built stores, and I've custom-coded ecommerce pages and automated email templates for clients. Using a different platform? [Ask me](/#start) and I'll tell you honestly if I'm the right fit.

## How I work

1. **Research:** analytics, customer behavior, competitors and your best-selling products.
2. **Fix the leaks:** product pages, checkout, speed and trust, starting with the changes most likely to lift sales.
3. **Set up email flows:** the automated emails that recover lost sales and bring customers back.
4. **Scale with ads:** once the store converts well, ads bring in more buyers.
5. **Report monthly:** revenue, conversion rate, email and ad results, and next month's plan.
