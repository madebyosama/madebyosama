I plan, design and edit your social media content, so your business shows up consistently and looks the part. Social media plans start at **$400 a month**, covering strategy, post design, video editing and publishing.

## What does social media marketing include?

Everything you need to post consistently without doing it yourself:

- **Strategy and content calendar:** what to post, where and when, based on your customers and your goals, planned a month ahead.
- **Post design:** on-brand static posts, carousels and stories that stop the scroll and are easy to read on a phone.
- **Video editing:** Reels, TikToks and YouTube Shorts edited from your clips, with captions, music and motion graphics.
- **Captions and hashtags:** written in your brand's voice, with a clear call to action.
- **Scheduling and publishing:** posts go out on time, every time.
- **Paid social (optional):** Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram to reach new customers. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta.
- **Monthly report:** reach, engagement, followers and clicks, and what we'll change next month.

## Which platforms do you cover?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts. Most small businesses do best starting with two platforms where their customers already spend time, then adding more once the routine is working.

## Can I get post design or video editing on its own?

Yes. If you already manage your own accounts and just need designs or edited videos, I offer design-only and editing-only work. [Tell me what you need](/#start) and I'll send a quote.

## How I work

1. **Research:** your brand, your customers and what competitors post, so the content has a clear angle.
2. **Plan:** a monthly calendar you approve before anything is designed.
3. **Design and edit:** posts and videos shared in one place, with feedback through a dedicated Slack channel.
4. **Publish and report:** everything goes live on schedule, and each month you see what worked.

## Why does social media design matter?

People decide in about a second whether to stop on a post. Clean, consistent design makes your business look established and trustworthy, even if you're small. It also makes your content recognizable, so people start to remember you. If your brand needs work first, I also do [branding and brand positioning](/branding).

## Questions

### How much does social media marketing cost?

Plans start at $400 per month. The price depends on how many platforms you want and how many posts and videos you need each month. After a short call you get a fixed monthly quote.

### Do I need to film the videos myself?

Usually, yes: short clips from your phone are enough. Send me the raw footage and I'll turn it into polished, captioned videos. If you don't have footage, I can make videos from photos, stock footage and motion graphics.

### How many posts do I get each month?

It depends on your plan and is agreed up front, so you always know what's coming. A common starting point is three to four posts a week across two platforms.

### Do you run social media ads?

Yes. I set up and manage Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta and is separate from my fee.

### Will the posts match my brand?

Yes. Every design follows your colors, fonts and tone of voice. If you don't have brand guidelines yet, I'll create simple ones before we start.
