I help small businesses figure out what makes them different, then turn it into a brand that looks credible everywhere: on your website, on social media and in person. Branding is priced per project, based on what you need.

## What is brand positioning?

Brand positioning is the clear answer to "why should I choose you over everyone else?" It defines who you serve, what you're best at and how you want to be seen, in a way your customers instantly understand.

Good positioning makes all your marketing easier. Your website, ads and social posts stop sounding like everyone else's, because they're built on a clear message.

## What does branding include?

- **Business and customer research:** interviews, reviews and competitor analysis to find the problem you solve better than anyone.
- **Positioning and messaging:** your positioning statement, key messages, tagline and tone of voice.
- **Logo and visual identity:** logo, color palette, typography and visual style.
- **Brand guidelines:** a simple guide that shows how to use your brand, so everything stays consistent.
- **Social media templates:** ready-to-use post and story templates in your new style.
- **Brand rollout:** applying the new brand to your [website](/website-design) and [social media](/social-media-marketing).

## Who is branding for?

New businesses that need to look established from day one, and existing businesses that have grown, changed direction or simply look dated. If people say "I didn't know you did that" or you keep competing on price, positioning is usually the problem.

## Questions

### How much does branding cost?

Branding is priced per project, based on what you need, from positioning and messaging only to a full visual identity. [Tell me about your business](/#start) and I'll send a quote.

### Do I need branding before a new website?

Not always, but it helps. A clear position and visual identity make your website, social media and ads more consistent and more convincing. If you're planning both, doing branding first saves rework.

### Can you refresh my existing logo instead of replacing it?

Yes. If your current brand has recognition, a refresh keeps what works and modernizes the rest.

### What files will I receive?

Your logo in all the formats you need for print, web and social, your color and font details, brand guidelines and any templates included in the project.

### How long does a branding project take?

It depends on the scope. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote, before we start.
