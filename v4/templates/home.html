Questions, answered

The things people usually ask before we start.

How much does a website cost?
Landing pages start at $1,300 and multi-page websites at $3,000. After a quick look at what you need, you get a fixed price up front, with no hidden fees and no subscription.
How long does it take?
A landing page is usually live in about two weeks. Multi-page websites take two to six weeks, depending on how many pages there are and how ready your content is.
How do we get started?
Fill in the project form, message me on WhatsApp or book a free call, and tell me about your business and your current site. I reply within a few hours.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes, that's most of what I do. I rebuild slow, cluttered sites so they load fast, work on every screen and are easy to update, and your existing content comes across.
Will my site be fast and show up on Google?
Every site is built mobile-first, with compressed images and clean code, and tested on Google PageSpeed before launch. Each page gets proper titles, descriptions and structured data so search engines understand it. Nobody honest can promise rankings, but Saeed Visa Consultants went from a PageSpeed score of 41 to 96.
Can I update the site myself?
Yes. Multi-page websites come with a CMS, usually WordPress, so you can change text, images and posts without waiting on a developer.
How many revisions do I get?
I don't count them. You see the design in Figma before anything is built and give feedback at every step, and we refine it together until you're happy.
I already have a design. Can you build it?
Yes. Send me the Figma file and I'll build it responsive, fast and true to the design, in WordPress or whatever fits your project.
Do you work with agencies?
Yes. Agencies have trusted me with design and WordPress builds for their clients for over five years, and I'm happy to work under your brand.
What happens after launch?
The site is yours. If you need changes, new pages or help later on, I'm a message away.
