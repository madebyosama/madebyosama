## The client

Primos Auto Repair is an independent repair shop competing with chains and dealerships for local customers.

## The problem

Their previous website was a basic template. It didn't show what made the shop different, and it didn't make it easy for customers to book.

## What I did

- **A strong visual identity** that feels approachable and confident, like the shop itself.
- **A booking form right in the hero**, so customers can request a service without hunting for it.
- **Clear service breakdowns** that answer "do you fix this?" at a glance.
- **Local SEO basics** so nearby drivers can find them.
