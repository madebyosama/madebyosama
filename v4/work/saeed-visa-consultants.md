## The client

Saeed Visa Consultants helps people study and work in Canada, the USA and Europe. People come to them for one of the biggest decisions of their lives, so the website has to earn their trust before they ever pick up the phone.

## The problem

The old site took more than six seconds to load and scored 41 on Google PageSpeed. Visa types were hard to tell apart, and on a phone the contact options were buried. Visitors left before they reached a page that answered their question.

## What I did

- **Rebuilt it from scratch** instead of patching the old theme, so there were no leftover plugins slowing every page down.
- **One clear page per visa type**, each written around the questions people actually ask and ending with the same simple next step: book a consultation.
- **Trust on every screen**: client success stories and contact details that are always one tap away.
- **Speed as a feature**: compressed images, lean code and nothing loading that the page doesn't need, on a WordPress setup the team can still edit themselves.

## The result

The site now loads in 1.4 seconds and scores 96 on PageSpeed. More importantly, consultation requests tripled, because people can find the right service and contact the team from any page.
