## The client

BLC Solicitors is a UK law firm covering immigration, family and commercial law. Most of their clients find them online, often on a phone and often under stress.

## The problem

The old website was outdated and didn't reflect how professional the firm is. Services were hard to find, the site struggled on mobile, and it wasn't pulling its weight in search.

## What I did

- **A calm, professional design** that says "you're in good hands" at first glance.
- **Clear service pages** with a simple structure, so people find their legal area in one or two taps.
- **Calls to action on every page**, so getting in touch is never more than a scroll away.
- **SEO from the ground up**: clean headings, fast pages and proper titles and descriptions on every page.

## The result

The new site loads twice as fast, mobile traffic grew 65%, and the bounce rate fell by 40%.
