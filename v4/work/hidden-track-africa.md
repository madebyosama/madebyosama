## The client

Hidden Track Africa plans tailor-made luxury safaris across East Africa, with offices in Kenya, the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Their trips are personal and expensive, so the website has to feel just as considered.

## The problem

A safari is a big purchase, made on trust. The site needed to show the experience, not just list packages, and make it easy to ask about a trip from anywhere in the world.

## What I did

- **Designed every page around the trip**, letting the destinations do the selling with nothing cluttering them.
- **Customised every detail** to the brand instead of settling for theme defaults.
- **Made enquiring effortless**, with a short form and contact options on every page.
- **Stayed on after launch** to handle changes and extra scope as the business grew.

## The result

Enquiries rose 40% in the three months after launch.
