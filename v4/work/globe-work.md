## The client

Globe Work connects businesses with skilled workers from around the world.

## The problem

Workforce supply is a trust business with a complicated offer. The site had to explain the service, show global reach and generate qualified leads from HR managers and business owners.

## What I did

- **A clear messaging hierarchy**: what they do, who it's for and how it works, in that order.
- **Detailed service pages** for each type of role.
- **Calls to action placed where decisions happen**, not just in the footer.
- **A design that balances corporate and warm**, so it appeals to both HR teams and owners.
